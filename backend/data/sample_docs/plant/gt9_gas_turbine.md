# Asset record: Gas turbine GT-9

Gas turbine GT-9 is installed at Northgate Plant and runs as a base-load unit.
GT-9 uses the B-310 thrust bearing on the compressor end.
GT-9 uses the S-55 shaft seal between the compressor and the turbine casing.
Vibration alarms on GT-9 trigger above 6.4 mm/s. The last major overhaul of GT-9 was completed in 2023.
