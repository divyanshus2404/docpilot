# Asset record: Gas turbine GT-7

Gas turbine GT-7 is installed at Riverside Plant and runs as a peaking unit.
GT-7 uses the B-220 thrust bearing on the compressor end.
GT-7 uses the S-40 shaft seal between the compressor and the turbine casing.
Vibration alarms on GT-7 trigger above 7.1 mm/s. The last major overhaul of GT-7 was completed in 2024.
