# Northwind Consulting: IT Security Policy

## Passwords and authentication
Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and must be changed every 90 days. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for email, VPN and all client systems.

## Devices
Only company-issued laptops may be used to access client data. Laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled and must lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity.

## Using AI tools
Employees must not paste client data into public AI chatbots. Approved internal AI assistants may be used for client work only after the engagement partner has signed off.

## Reporting incidents
Any suspected security incident, including a lost laptop or a phishing email, must be reported to the security team at security@northwind.example within 1 hour.
