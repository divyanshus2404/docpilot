# Northwind Consulting: Travel & Expense Policy

Northwind Consulting is a fictional company used to demo DocPilot.

## Booking travel
All business travel must be booked through the internal travel portal at least 7 days in advance. Economy class is the default for flights under 6 hours. Business class may be approved by a partner for flights longer than 6 hours.

## Daily allowance
Employees travelling within India receive a daily meal allowance of INR 1,500. For international travel the daily allowance is USD 60. Alcohol is not reimbursable.

## Hotels
Hotel stays are capped at INR 6,000 per night in metro cities and INR 4,000 per night in other cities. Employees should prefer hotels from the approved vendor list.

## Submitting expenses
Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the trip end date, with itemised receipts for any single expense above INR 500. Claims older than 60 days will not be reimbursed.
