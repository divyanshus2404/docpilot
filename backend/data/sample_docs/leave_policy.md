# Northwind Consulting: Leave Policy

## Annual leave
Full-time employees earn 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, accrued monthly at 2 days per month. Up to 10 unused days can be carried forward to the next year.

## Sick leave
Employees get 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for sick leave longer than 2 consecutive days.

## Interns
Interns earn 1 day of paid leave per month of internship. Interns are not eligible to carry forward unused leave.

## Parental leave
Primary caregivers receive 26 weeks of paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 4 weeks of paid parental leave, which must be taken within 6 months of the birth or adoption.
